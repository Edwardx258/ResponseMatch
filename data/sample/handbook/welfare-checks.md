# Welfare checks

A welfare check confirms that a person is safe. Volunteers knock, identify themselves and
wait. They do not force entry. If there is no answer and there are signs of an emergency,
such as someone calling for help, they call 911 instead of going in.

Pair volunteers for welfare checks when possible, and prefer volunteers who speak the
resident's language.
